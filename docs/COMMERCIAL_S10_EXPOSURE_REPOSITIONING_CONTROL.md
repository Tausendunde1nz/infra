## Prepared but inactive

The application contains a central bilingual copy/design SSOT, a script-free
and form-free SFW preview landing page, a deterministic button-first bot
persona, twelve weeks of SFW content inventory, opt-in nurture copy, opaque
referrals, aggregate-only analytics and synthetic future experience modes.

The modes are deliberately not a live Adult workflow. The contract keeps real
media, identity documents, AVS, payments, external publishing and real users
closed. Consent preview and payment boundaries are design constraints only.

The offline privacy review closed three concrete gaps before this binding:
analytics dimensions are now allowlisted at event construction, consent flags
and synthetic destinations are exact and fail-closed, and persona/mode inputs
are validated before state is returned. The implementation modules and review
evidence are individually SHA-256-bound in the manifest. Real destinations and
sensitive preference profiles remain unavailable.

are discovery evidence, not registration, availability or legal clearance.

similarity search, legal trademark clearance, domain ownership, exact social
handle ownership, public rename approval and approved imprint/privacy copy.

service-address information. A bare forwarding address or c/o construct is not
treated as cleared. Any address arrangement requires German legal review and a
genuine, usable establishment or service-address basis.
