WHY THE CYCLE IS REMOVED, NOT HIDDEN
R1 required full writer exclusion/backup before stopping exactly the three
actors whose known Docker delegation prevents that exclusion. R3 separates
STOP-ONLY BOOTSTRAP ADMISSION from MUTATION/BACKUP ADMISSION. The former uses
trusted pre-existing Source/operator/PID1 identities and a READ WITNESS, not
the later backup, probes, installed helper or successful stop. Full exclusion
is obtained/verified AFTER quiescence and BEFORE opt staging or any backup
called protected. Installation requires that backup and its successful restore.
Owner preparation requires actual rights acceptance and its OWN fresh backup.
No phase depends on a later success as evidence of its own admission.
